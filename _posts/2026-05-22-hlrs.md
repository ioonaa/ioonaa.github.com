<strong>theGATE</strong> is an initiative aimed at exploring the convergence of Science, Technology, Arts and 
Society.
It took place from May 18 to 22 at the <strong>High Performance Computing Center in Stuttgart</strong> (HLRS) and I was delighted to join the eCulture Convention to think about and learn...how physics can inspire art and how art can inspire physics...how we visualise phenomena that are impossible to see and contradict our very understanding of the world...what it means to read AI as African Intelligence and how important it is to achieve digital sovereignty...whether the cooling systems of super computers go through computational pain...
